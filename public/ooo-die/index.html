Portrait of a Two-Wide Mind

R10K-style out-of-order RISC-V core
4,088 lines of SystemVerilog, rendered as an imagined die
Canvas 2D · 1800 × 1240 logical px · seeded, deterministic

No fab ever saw this chip. The floorplan is invented, but each block is drawn with the logic it actually contains: adders and output muxes in the ALUs, a partial-product, shift and add slice in each of the four multiplier stages, tag comparators in the caches, wakeup comparators and priority selectors in the reservation station, a PLA in each decoder, and the load unit's state machine.

The two gold rails that loop the die are the common data bus, two results wide, carrying tags back to whoever is waiting. Look for the doodle in the lower right corner. Real chip designers have hidden drawings in their metal layers for decades.
