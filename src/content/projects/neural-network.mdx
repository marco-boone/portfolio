## DESIGN

Each robot in the swarm is controlled by its own small neural network. The layers, matrix math, and forward propagation are written by hand, with no ML libraries.

Every frame, the network reads the robot's velocity, its distance to the walls along two rays just off its heading, and how many robots are nearby. Its outputs weight steering vectors borrowed from the boids algorithm (alignment, cohesion, separation, and the robot's own heading), and the sum is applied to the robot as a force.

<NetworkLayers>7-5-4 network with sigmoid activations - every line is a weight in a hand-written matrix</NetworkLayers>

Instead of backpropagation, the networks are trained with a genetic algorithm. A robot dies if it hits a wall or stops moving, and its fitness is how long it stayed alive. Once the whole generation is dead, the two longest-lived robots are averaged into a "best brain", and the next generation is built from mutated copies of it plus some fresh random networks.

<GaLoop>the scene reloads for every generation</GaLoop>
