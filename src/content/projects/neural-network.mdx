## Overview

Each robot in the swarm is controlled by its own small neural network. The network reads the robot's sensors and outputs motor speeds. Instead of backpropagation, the networks are trained with a genetic algorithm: the best performers each generation are copied and mutated.

<NetworkLayers>Sensor readings go in, motor speeds come out. Every line is a weight in a hand-written matrix.</NetworkLayers>

## Training with a genetic algorithm

Every generation, each robot is scored on how well it does its task. The top robots survive, and their weights and biases are copied and randomly mutated to make the next generation.

<GaLoop>The training loop repeats until the swarm's behavior stops improving.</GaLoop>

## Key features

- Custom matrix math and forward propagation, no ML libraries
- Genetic algorithm mutates weights and biases each generation
- Fitness function rewards reaching the goal and penalizes collisions {/* FILLER */}
