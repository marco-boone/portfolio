## CONTEXT

This processor design was completed for my *capstone* project. A successful project meant designing, building, and  synthesizing a sufficently performant OoO (out-of-order) processor. 

## DESIGN

The core was inspired by the MIPS-R10K architecture, and thus we conceptually split our core into a front, middle, and back end such that the middle end housed the canon structures found in the R10K.
